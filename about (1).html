About Us

History

Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer, opened Next Level in Johannesburg in 2023. He saw that many gamers had no access to good equipment or organised competitions, so he built an arena for them.

Vision

To be the most loved gaming and esports venue in South Africa.

Mission

Give gamers of all ages top equipment, fair competition and a place to meet friends.

Goals

Host more tournaments, partner with more schools and open a second venue.
